# Incidents

Record operational failures that expose a reusable process, security, tooling, or verification gap. Ordinary product bugs belong in the issue tracker or local work record.

No incidents are currently recorded.

## Incident Template

### YYYY-MM-DD - Incident Title

- **Status:** Open | Mitigated | Resolved and verified
- **Impact:** What was blocked, exposed, lost, or made unreliable.
- **Evidence:** Logs, ids, commits, screenshots, or reproducible observations.
- **Diagnosis:** Confirmed cause and remaining uncertainty.
- **Resolution:** Actions taken without hiding destructive or manual state changes.
- **Verification:** How the resolution was proven.
- **Prevention:** Durable rule, automation, or documentation change.
